# What the fleet has actually made — 2026-09-22

Measured against the broker, not against Discord posts or anybody's memory.
Account `PA3VG5AP6TO5`, the one paper account every fleet bot shares.

## The headline is real

```
base value        $100,000.00
equity            $110,419.89
total P/L          +$10,419.89
```

The account is up about ten and a half thousand dollars. That part is true and
worth saying plainly, because the rest of this note is qualifications and they
should not be mistaken for "the bots do nothing".

## Almost none of it is banked

```
total unrealised  +$10,534   (41 open positions)
=> realised        ~-$114
```

Every dollar of the gain is sitting in open positions. Nothing has been sold
into it. An unrealised gain is a price, not a result — it is what the position
would be worth if it closed right now at the last print, and this fleet has a
documented history of positions that never close (`firm_i_memecoins_ii` held a
short for eleven days after it was killed, and only closed today).

## Three quarters of it rests on a cost basis that is not a price

```
equities + options   25 positions   +$2,735.96   (26%)
crypto               16 positions   +$7,798.23   (74%)
```

The equity side looks like ordinary trading: HOOD +17.5%, INTC +13.6%,
TSLA +22.5%. Plausible sizes, plausible gains. If the fleet has an edge, this
is where the evidence for it would live.

The crypto side does not:

| symbol | entry | now | "gain" |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARBUSD | $0.010510 | $0.2199 | +1992.6% |
| GRTUSD | $0.005839 | $0.0235 | +301.9% |
| DOTUSD | $0.255754 | $1.1850 | +363.3% |
| CRVUSD | $0.099753 | $0.3643 | +265.2% |
| BTCUSD | $40,625.93 | $85,405.30 | +110.2% |

ARB has never traded at a cent. GRT has never traded at half a cent. Those are
not entry prices, they are a broken cost basis, and a percentage computed from a
broken cost basis is not a return. The journal does not reconcile them either:
the ARBUSD records in `broker_journal.jsonl` carry an empty `side`, a negative
`qty` and `net_amount: 0`, which is not a fill.

So the +$7,798 is not evidence of profit. It is evidence that a number is wrong,
and it happens to be wrong in the flattering direction.

## What this does and does not say

It does **not** say the bots are worthless. ~$2,700 of plausible unrealised
equity gains across 25 positions is a real signal worth pursuing, and this note
takes no view on which bot produced it — that decomposition has not been done.

It does say that the number to quote is **not** "+$10.4k". Quoting that figure
means claiming the ARB cost basis, and the moment anyone checks, the whole
report loses its credibility along with the part that was true.

The honest sentence is: *a shared paper account is up $10.4k on paper, roughly
$2.7k of which is plausible, none of which is realised.*

## What to fix

1. Decompose P/L per bot. One shared account means no bot currently has a
   track record of its own — see the same problem in `project-shared-alpaca-account`.
2. Find out why the crypto cost basis is wrong before trusting any crypto number.
3. Prefer realised P/L in every report. Unrealised is a forecast.
