# Data use notice

This repository republishes normalized fields derived from U.S. congressional financial
disclosure reports made public by the Clerk of the House of Representatives and the
Secretary of the Senate.

The data is not offered under CC0 or an unrestricted open-data license. Under
5 U.S.C. 13107(c), it is unlawful to obtain or use a covered report:

1. for any unlawful purpose;
2. for any commercial purpose, other than by news and communications media for
   dissemination to the general public;
3. for determining or establishing the credit rating of any individual; or
4. directly or indirectly in the solicitation of money for any political, charitable,
   or other purpose.

The statute also authorizes a civil action by the Attorney General for prohibited use.
See the current official text:
https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=(title:5%20section:13107%20edition:prelim)

This notice is informational and is not legal advice. Users are responsible for
determining whether their use complies with applicable law and source-site terms.
