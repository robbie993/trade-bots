## 1. The goal

The village is a multi-agent paper-trading system: nine firm lineages evolve small
genomes and trade a shared universe on 15-minute bars. **The goal is not a profitable
bot. It is a process that makes false confidence hard.** A trustworthy negative result
is a win.

That framing earned itself today. Six separate conclusions were reached and then killed
by the next test — every one of them an artefact of the measurement rather than a fact
about the strategies. The apparatus is the project; the strategies are its test cases.

## 4. What changed, and the measurements behind it

**The village was booking fills that could not have happened.** 499 of 2,039 (24.5%)
landed when the US equity market was shut — EFA, XOM, JNJ, PG, NVDA — each at a price
nobody was quoting, each carrying invented P&L into the scores that decide which genome
survives. `session.py` + `paper.py` now refuse them. Verified live across the restart:
PG *filled* at 02:45 UTC on the old code and was *refused* at 03:00 on the new.

**Costs were guesses.** Measured from SIP quotes at the village's own fill timestamps:

```
equities   regular 0.82 bps/side   pre 1.55   after 5.14     (flat 5.00 assumed)
crypto     BTC 1.51  ETH 2.31  SOL 5.32                      (10 assumed)
           DOGE 19.64  WIF 19.65                             (35 assumed)
```

Fees stay at 25 bps for crypto — Alpaca's retail tier really is about that; only the
spread was invented. **The memecoin signal test is unblocked: the bar is 39 bps round
trip, not 120.**

**Evolution does not work.** This is the session's main result and it took four tests:

- `firm_b_stocks_ii_vi_ii`, the best genome, scores **+0.590 in-sample → −1.405
  held-out**. 0 of 6 surviving firms is positive out of sample.
- **Spearman(in-sample rank, holdout rank) = +0.056, t=+0.94, n=280.** The selection
  rule carries no out-of-sample information. Range across the 280 is 8.83 with sd 1.80,
  so this is not restriction of range — it is a reasonably tight bound on zero.
- The hand-picked defaults (SMA 10/30, RSI 14) score **−2.700**, *worse* than random
  genomes at −1.921. The conventional parameters are the most arbitraged points in the
  space. So the search is real; it simply has nothing to find.
- Random genomes on the **same window** as the holdout score −0.950 against the evolved
  firms' −1.318. Like for like, evolved is marginally *worse* than random.

`genome_holdout` + the per-generation rank test make this a standing readout instead of
28 minutes of archaeology.

## 5. What failed

**Six conclusions died in one day. Every one was a measurement artefact.**

1. *"No edge, costs kill it"* — the cost constant was ~9x too high. T\* flipped from
   NEVER to 2 bars on identical edge numbers.
2. *"The village is far worse than random (z=−8.2)"* — **one bar** did it. 22 firms
   bought DOGE and WIF into an overnight 5% slide. Drop it: −14.39 → −2.07.
3. *"Stock-picking deficit, t=−2.01"* — t=**+0.99** once clustered by bar. 1,017 entries
   sit on 239 bars.
4. *"Evolution beats random by 0.9"* — comparing a 216-bar window against a 720-bar one.
   `max_drawdown` only grows with the window. Equal-length windows put the regime
   component at **+0.051**; the rest was length.
5. *"Best genome scores +0.425"* — in-sample. Holdout −1.405.
6. *"Evolution works"* — Spearman +0.056.

**Bugs I introduced and caught:**

- Migration 024 drafted as three bare `ALTER TABLE ADD COLUMN`. Migration 022 explains
  at length that SQLite has no `IF NOT EXISTS` for that and every migration re-runs on
  `init-db`. It is a side table now.
- The first suite run after wiring the ledger wrote `evolver:alpha`, `evolver:beta`,
  `evolver:test_firm` into the **real** ledger — fixtures inflating the look count that
  decides whether a real result means anything. Three rows removed; `conftest` now
  points it at tmp.
- Adding genes without `BASE_GENOME` defaults threw `KeyError: 'rsi_entry'` on *every*
  file, surfacing as "could not be read" — a message pointing at the file, not the
  missing default.
- Restarted the trader on the wrong Python (see §2).
- Two of my own tests asserted things the code was right to refuse.

**Things I claimed and had to retract:** that the fitness cost model was "40x" wrong
(only two crypto desks carry overrides; equities were ~9x); that the court's
`prosecution`/`defence` columns were broken (they hold prose — `ROUND()` on text returns
0.0, my query); that "the scan is done" after reading ~1-2% of it.

**Known-unfixed:** my holdout had **no purge gap**. `value_window` reaches 150 bars
against a 216-bar holdout, so up to 69% of holdout bars compute indicators from training
bars. Leakage inflates correlation, so ρ=+0.056 is if anything *understated* and the null
stands — but every future holdout needs a gap ≥ max lookback.

## 6. What to do next

**1. Build a reversion analyst.** This is the blocker. The genome vocabulary now
*accepts* `rsi_entry` / `ibs_entry` / `pullback_atr` — the court stopped charging VERITAS
25 points for declaring them (158.06 → 133.78) — but **no analyst reads them**. `grep`
says `rsi_entry` appears in exactly one module: `evolver.py`, where it is defined. So
the backtest still runs the village's generic strategy and ignores those numbers, and
the resulting verdict is not a verdict on VERITAS. VERITAS is the one strategy in the
fleet with real positive evidence (5/5 pre-registered criteria, p<0.002 vs 500
random-entry runs). Until this exists, the village cannot evaluate it.

Note `lookback` and `max_positions` are different — already read by `market_data.py` and
`risk_manager.py`, so those two bite already.

**2. Decide on the uncommitted vocabulary work.** It is sound and tested-by-running but
half-finished without #1. Commit it as groundwork or hold it.

**3. Purge gap on every holdout.** ≥ max lookback (currently 150).

**4. Benchmark hurdle in fitness.** The single largest correctness gap. `fitness =
return_pct − maxDD/2` has **no benchmark**: a firm returning +0.5% while SPY does +10%
scores positive. `/Users/robbie/trade-bots-hive/hive_mind/lock.py:670` already solved
this — *"Both hurdles, not either. Beating the index alone is cleared by sitting in cash
through a falling market; beating cash alone was never enough to justify the risk of
being here."*

**5. Stop evolving harder.** More generations through a rule with ρ=0.056 produces
better-looking headline numbers and no more truth.

**6. Then the signal branches** — explicit-call extraction on `i_memecoins` (the only
desk with zero contaminated fills, and the exact asset class Haase's crowd-signal effect
is strongest on), on-chain analyst stub, everything else via `ScannerSpec`.
